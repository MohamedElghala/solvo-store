Returns & Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

🛡️

30-DAY MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE

If you're not 100% satisfied with your SOLVO purchase, return it within 30 days for a full refund. No questions asked.

Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return, the following conditions must be met:

  • The return request must be made within 30 days of receiving the item.
  • The item must be unused, unworn, and in its original condition.
  • The item must be in its original packaging with all tags and accessories included.
  • You must have proof of purchase (order confirmation email or order number).

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items that have been used, installed, or show signs of wear
  • Items damaged due to misuse or negligence by the customer
  • Items returned after the 30-day return window

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact Us: Email support@solvo.store with your order number and reason for return.
  2. Receive Authorization: We will respond within 24–48 hours with a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and return instructions.
  3. Ship the Item: Pack the item securely in its original packaging and ship it to the address provided. Include the RMA number inside the package.
  4. Receive Your Refund: Once we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund will be processed.

Return Shipping Costs

  • Change of Mind / No Longer Want: The customer is responsible for return shipping costs.
  • Defective / Damaged / Wrong Item: SOLVO will cover the return shipping cost and send a free replacement or issue a full refund (including original shipping costs).

Refund Processing

  • Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days after we receive the returned item and confirm it meets our return conditions.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method (PayPal or card used at checkout).
  • You will receive an email confirmation when your refund has been processed.
  • Please allow an additional 3–5 business days for the refund to appear in your account, depending on your financial institution.

Defective or Damaged Items

If you receive a defective or damaged item:

  1. Contact us within 7 days of delivery at support@solvo.store.
  2. Include photos of the damage or defect and your order number.
  3. We will offer you a free replacement shipped at our expense, or a full refund — your choice.

Exchanges

We do not currently offer direct exchanges. If you need a different quantity (e.g., upgrading from a single unit to the 2-pack), please initiate a return for the original order and place a new order for the desired product.

Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received your refund within the expected timeframe:

  1. Check your bank account or PayPal balance again.
  2. Contact your credit card company or bank — refunds may take additional processing time.
  3. If you've done both and still have not received your refund, contact us at support@solvo.store.

Contact

For return and refund inquiries, email us at support@solvo.store. We respond within 24 hours.
