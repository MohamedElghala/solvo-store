What is the SOLVO SeatGap Organizer?

The SOLVO SeatGap Organizer™ is a premium automotive accessory that fills the gap between your car seat and center console. It prevents phones, keys, coins, and other small items from falling into the hard-to-reach crevice. Our model also features built-in retractable USB-C and Lightning fast-charging cables, so you can charge your devices without messy cords.

Does it fit my vehicle?

The SOLVO SeatGap Organizer is designed with a universal adjustable width that fits 99% of vehicles, including sedans, SUVs, trucks, and sports cars. It's compatible with BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Tesla, Toyota, Ford, Honda, Hyundai, Kia, Porsche, Audi, Land Rover, Chevrolet, and 50+ other makes. The high-density memory foam base compresses to fit gaps ranging from 0.6" to 2.4" wide. Use our Fitment Checker on the homepage to verify your specific vehicle.

How do I install it?

Installation takes about 10 seconds — no tools required. Simply slide the organizer into the gap between your seat and center console. The non-slip memory foam base naturally wedges itself in place and holds firmly. No adhesives, clips, or modifications to your car interior are needed. It can be removed just as easily.

Does it really have USB-C charging built in?

Yes! The SOLVO SeatGap Organizer features dual retractable charging cables — one USB-C and one Lightning — built directly into the unit. Simply connect the main power cable to your car's USB port (or cigarette lighter with an adapter), and the retractable cables are ready to charge your phone at fast-charging speeds. The cables retract neatly when not in use, keeping your cabin clutter-free.

What material is it made from?

The exterior features a premium aerospace-grade carbon fiber textured finish combined with Nappa leather accents. It's designed to match the look and feel of luxury OEM car interiors (BMW, Mercedes, Tesla, etc.). The material is scratch-resistant, heat-resistant (-22°F to 176°F), and spill-proof — just wipe clean with any interior cleaner.

Is it compatible with heated seats?

Yes. The SOLVO SeatGap Organizer is rated for temperatures from -22°F to 176°F (-30°C to 80°C) and is fully compatible with heated seats. The memory foam base and exterior materials will not melt, deform, or emit odors when exposed to seat heating elements.
